Okay so yesterday, PHP got two critical bugs that let anyone run code on your server without even logging in 🫩.. The details leaked before the patch was even out... It's like pelli lo bojanaalu laaga🦥...! It works on WordPress, MediaWiki, Magento, Joomla and Laravel, so basically half the internet, and Censys says around 1.5 million servers are running a version with no fix.

Then every agency you can think of said the same thing. NSA, FBI, CISA, the UK and CERT-EU all went "take your system offline." And then the Prime Minister of Japan posted at 5:55 in the morning telling 48.8 million people to do the same. Ma amma kuda doesn't wake me up with that much urgency.

The Dutch cybersecurity agency took its own website down, which is like the watchman locking himself out of the house. The Dutch parliament and the Port of Rotterdam went offline, Finland, France and Denmark started pulling government sites, and Wikipedia, Archive.org, Asana, GOG and Something Awful all went down. The University of Vienna's webmail also went down, for no reason..

And that's just PHP. Part 1 only braaathers...

GitLab dropped a CVSS 10.0, the highest score they give, for a bug that's been sitting there since 2014... . TWELVE YEARS 🙄.. Every self-hosted GitLab... Then LiteLLM 1.107.0 got hijacked and shipped a credential stealer and a remote access trojan in every wheel and the Docker images, including latest. You didn't even have to import it, installing was enough...

Railway paused every deployment on the whole platform because someone got into the environment variables, so now everyone has to rotate every secret they ever stored there. TiDB Cloud said someone was wandering around inside with stolen credentials for about a month, and an extortion group claims they took 10.6 TB. Zeabur got breached for the second time in two months and is shutting down completely, which is a more dramatic exit than any Bigg Boss elimination!

Also then... Node.js patched remote code execution and a TLS bypass, CISA says Plex is being actively exploited, someone took over 300 Tor exit relays to steal SSH passwords, SafePay ransomware has hit over 600 critical infrastructure orgs, and India's biggest bank filed a data loss incident

But the best one and el clasico one is that emudhra one.. emudhra is a certificate authority that has issued over 60 million certificates. Its entire job is proving that websites are who they say they are. Its private keys were supposed to be random numbers over a hundred digits long. One of them was 4.... WTH just 4 aaa... The others were 66 and 237... My ATM PIN has more security than this emo.. 🤧!
